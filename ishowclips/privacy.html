Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

IShowClips ("the app") is a personal automation tool used solely by its operator to upload short-form video clips to their own YouTube channel.

Information we access

The app uses Google OAuth with the youtube.upload scope for the single purpose of uploading video files to the account holder's own YouTube channel. The app does not read, collect, store, sell, or share any personal information, contacts, messages, or viewing data.

Data storage

The only credential stored is a Google OAuth token, kept privately on the operator's own server and used exclusively to perform uploads. No user data is transmitted to third parties.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
