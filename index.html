"From molecular biology to game mechanics — I've always been fascinated by the systems that drive human experience."

I'm James Sypherd — game designer, voice actor, stuntman, and biomedical engineer. My graduate work at Arizona State University trained me to think rigorously about complex systems, but my creative passions pulled me toward the most intricate system of all: human storytelling.

I design games that blend analytical thinking with emotional depth — from Roblox dodgeball arenas modeled on real university campuses, to tabletop RPGs where one player becomes the villain, to 2D roguelikes built in Godot. When I step in front of a camera or a microphone, I bring that same precision to inhabiting a character.

My biomedical engineering background isn't separate from my creative work — it's woven through it. I've built motion capture conversion tools, voiced fictional horrors for indie games, trained in stunt performance, and designed gameplay mechanics grounded in real biomechanics. The thread running through all of it is a belief that the most memorable experiences happen when science and storytelling meet.

The Games

Featured Title
Video Game · Roblox & UEFN
Butterwood

A dodgeball game built to faithfully simulate the Arizona State University campus — giving players a sports experience rooted in a place they know. Core game mechanics including zone closure systems inspired by battle royale design and spawn point architecture. Simultaneously developed in Unreal Editor for Fortnite (UEFN) as sole designer.

In Development Roblox · Fortnite UEFN

Roguelike · Godot 4
Thunderwalker 2D

A creepy 2D dungeon crawler inspired by Inscryption aesthetics. Hunt mythological creatures through 4 interconnected rooms, facing 3 enemy types and a Wendigo boss with 3-phase AI. Real-time combat with health management and procedural difficulty scaling.

Godot 4 · GDScript GitHub →
Fitness Game · Unity 6
Imagine

A sisyphean exercise game suite that ties rowing machines, cycling, and strength training to engaging game narratives. Integrates with Concept2 PM5 SDK and BLE heart rate monitors for real biometric feedback tied to gameplay progression.

Unity 6 · C# GitHub →
Tabletop RPG · Card Game
Hunting Grounds

A cooperative RPG card game for four players in the vein of Gloomhaven — with a twist. One rotating player takes on the role of the villain each combat. The debut campaign pits hunters against Dracula himself, with a system designed to support future expansion packs.

4 Players · Expandable Original
Software · Engineering Tool
C3D → FBX Mocap Converter

A conversion tool that transforms motion capture data from the biomechanics-standard .c3d format into .fbx files compatible with Unreal Engine 5's MetaHuman retargeting pipeline. Designed to help children's hospitals gamify biomechanics capture sessions.

"The most compelling characters live at the intersection of the uncanny and the deeply human."

For James, acting is a natural extension of his design sensibility. Where game design asks "how will a player move through this world?" — performance asks "how does this person move through theirs?" The analytical instincts sharpen the craft. The craft keeps the analysis human.

From voicing one of literature's most iconic horror writers to training in stunt performance, James brings a precise, physical, and character-driven approach to every role.
