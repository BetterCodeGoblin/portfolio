"From molecular biology to game mechanics — I've always been fascinated by the systems that drive human experience."

I'm James Sypherd — game designer, voice actor, and biomedical engineer. My graduate work at Arizona State University trained me to think rigorously about complex systems, but my creative passions pulled me toward the most intricate system of all: human storytelling.

I design games that blend analytical thinking with emotional depth — from Roblox dodgeball arenas modeled on real university campuses, to tabletop RPGs where one player becomes the villain, to 2D roguelikes built in Godot. When I step in front of a camera or a microphone, I bring that same precision to inhabiting a character.

My biomedical engineering background isn't separate from my creative work — it's woven through it. I've built motion capture conversion tools, voiced fictional horrors for indie games, and designed gameplay mechanics grounded in real biomechanics. The thread running through all of it is a belief that the most memorable experiences happen when science and storytelling meet.

Screen & Voice

✦ ✦ ✦

"The most compelling characters live at the intersection of the uncanny and the deeply human."

For James, acting is a natural extension of his design sensibility. Where game design asks "how will a player move through this world?" — performance asks "how does this person move through theirs?" The analytical instincts sharpen the craft. The craft keeps the analysis human.

From voicing one of literature's most iconic horror writers to character-driven performance work, James brings a precise, physical, and emotionally grounded approach to every role.
